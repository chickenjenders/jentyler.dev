Welcome to my secret corner of the internet. This is where thoughts get to exist without judgment—raw, unfiltered, just _me_.

I've been wanting a space like this for a while. Not a public blog, not social media where you curate your best self. Just a place to write when the feeling strikes, to ramble about ideas that aren't fully formed yet, or to just... think out loud.

This is that space now.
